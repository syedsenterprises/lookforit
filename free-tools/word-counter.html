Word Counter & Readability

Paste any text for word and character counts, reading time, and an approximate readability score.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type on this page is sent to any server, stored, or tracked. Close the tab and it is gone.

Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute. The Flesch score is an approximation from syllable estimates, not a precise grade.
