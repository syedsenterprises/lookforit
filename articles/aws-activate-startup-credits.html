AWS Activate Credits for Startups Explained

AWS Activate startup credits: the headline amount, what credits cover including Bedrock models, and how to prepare an application.

What AWS Activate is

AWS Activate is Amazon’s startup program, part of AWS for Startups, which says it supports over 350,000 startups globally with technical guidance, business mentorship, and go-to-market resources. The headline offer on AWS’s official page: apply to receive up to $200,000 in AWS Activate Credits to offset costs on infrastructure, data services, and AI/ML models.

Related listing in our directory: AWS Bedrock.

What the credits cover

According to AWS, credits offset costs on infrastructure, data services, and AI/ML models, and are redeemable on third-party models on Amazon Bedrock. That last point matters for AI startups: you can spend credits on models from other providers served through Bedrock, not only on Amazon’s own models. AWS also states that AI startups ready to grow may be eligible for additional credits beyond Activate.

Beyond credits, AWS describes co-build, co-market, and co-sell support through the AWS Partner Network, which it says has more than 140,000 partners from over 200 countries. Members also get technical guidance and business mentorship from the AWS for Startups team, which includes former founders, CTOs, venture capitalists, angel investors, and mentors.

Tiers and eligibility: check the official page

Be careful with tier figures: blogs and guides quote several different Activate tiers and amounts, and the numbers have changed over time. The only figure stated on the official AWS page we checked is up to $200,000 in Activate Credits, with additional credits possible for AI startups ready to scale. Before you plan runway around a specific tier, confirm the current tier terms, eligibility rules, and expiry periods on the official page — do not rely on a guide’s table.

As preparation, have the basics ready before you apply: a functioning company website that explains what you build, an AWS account, and a clear description of your product and stage. Applications are reviewed individually, and AWS states acceptance is at its discretion.

Using credits wisely

Credits are runway, not free infrastructure forever. They expire, and the architecture you build on them becomes the bill you pay afterwards. Practical habits: set billing alerts from day one, tag resources by project so you can see burn, and keep the parts of your system that are easy to move portable. That way the end of credits is a planned step, not a surprise.

Frequently asked questions

What is AWS Activate?

AWS Activate is Amazon’s startup program within AWS for Startups, offering credits, technical guidance, business mentorship, and go-to-market resources to early-stage and growth-stage startups.

How much can startups receive in AWS Activate Credits?

According to AWS’s official page, qualifying startups can apply for up to $200,000 in AWS Activate Credits, and AI startups ready to grow may be eligible for additional credits beyond Activate.

Do Activate Credits cover third-party models on Bedrock?

Yes. AWS states that Activate Credits are redeemable on third-party models on Amazon Bedrock, as well as on infrastructure, data services, and AI/ML models.

Are the tier amounts the same for every startup?

No. Tier terms, eligibility rules, and expiry periods change over time and vary by startup. Confirm the current terms on the official AWS Activate page before planning around a specific amount.

What should I prepare before applying?

Have a functioning company website that explains what you build, an AWS account, and a clear description of your product and stage. Applications are reviewed individually.

Do credits last forever?

No. Credits expire, so treat them as runway: set billing alerts, track burn by project, and plan for normal AWS billing when credits end.
