Gamma vs SlidesAI: AI Presentations Compared

Gamma vs SlidesAI compared by destination: shareable web decks, native Google Slides, and branded materials, plus the workflow that makes either work.

Start with the destination, not the tool

Gamma and SlidesAI serve different destinations, and the destination decides the winner. Gamma is a web app: you generate cards from a prompt, outline, or document, shape them with AI, and share a polished link that also works as a page. SlidesAI is an add-on workflow: it works inside Google Slides or PowerPoint, so the final file lives where your team already edits. This comparison is framed by destination, editing workflow, and output format, not by a single score.

Gamma for shareable, designed decks

Choose Gamma when the deck must look polished as a link: pitches to outsiders, public pages, and visual stories. Its card-based layouts, themes, and web sharing suit audiences who will view rather than edit. Check exported PowerPoint layout before delivery if the deck must also live as a file, since web-first designs do not always translate perfectly.

SlidesAI for Google Slides natives

Choose SlidesAI when the final file must live in Google Slides with minimal switching: lessons, team decks, and anything colleagues will edit. Because it works inside the presentation tool, there are no export headaches, and the deck stays editable in the format your team knows. Its designs tend to be simpler, so expect a manual polish pass for client-facing work.

Canva when the deck is part of branded materials

Canva AI is the third option worth testing when the deck sits inside wider branded materials: social graphics, one-pagers, and templates in one place. Its strength is the template and asset library, not pure AI drafting. Whichever tool you pick, work outline-first: approve the outline, then generate, then edit text, density, and fonts before presenting.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI presentation maker?

It depends on the destination: Gamma for polished shareable web decks, SlidesAI for native Google Slides files, and Canva when the deck is part of wider branded materials.

Is Gamma or SlidesAI better?

Gamma is better for designed, shareable decks viewed as links. SlidesAI is better when the final file must live in Google Slides with no export friction.

Can AI make a whole deck from one prompt?

It can draft one, but a presentable deck still needs an approved outline, edited text, and a design pass. Treat AI output as a first draft, not a finished deck.

Will AI slides work in PowerPoint?

SlidesAI output lives in Google Slides and exports to PowerPoint. Gamma exports to PowerPoint too, but web-first layouts may need checking after export.

Do AI presentation tools have free plans?

Both Gamma and SlidesAI offer free tiers with limits on volume and features. Test the full workflow, including export, on the free tier before paying.

How do I fix AI slides that look generic?

Tighten the outline first, cut text density per slide, use your own examples and images, and apply one consistent theme instead of accepting every default.
