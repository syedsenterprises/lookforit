About

What is LookForIt?

LookForIt is an AI tools directory and discovery platform focused on practical utility — helping founders, freelancers, marketers, developers, and creators find artificial intelligence tools they can use immediately for real tasks.

How we review AI tools

We evaluate tools by use-case fit, output quality, workflow value, reliability, and accessibility for everyday users. Listings are organized into clear categories so visitors can compare options faster and choose tools that match their goals.

Why use our directory

Instead of opening dozens of random tabs, visitors can use one structured directory to discover trusted tools for productivity, coding, design, content, automation, and business growth. This saves research time and improves decision quality.

Who runs LookForIt?

LookForIt is independently run by Shahid. The site is not affiliated with any AI company or tool provider listed in the directory. Our goal is to provide honest, unbiased descriptions of each tool based on publicly available information.

Our promise: no invented tools, ratings, prices, or review counts. If we could not verify something, we say so. A listing is never an endorsement — always trial a paid tool before committing.
